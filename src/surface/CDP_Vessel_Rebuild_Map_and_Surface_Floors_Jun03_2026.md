## The sequence and the cadence

Order: profile capture first, because it is the input that personalises the card, the reading, and compatibility; then the year and cosmic context; then the tiers and Stripe, since they unblock commerce; then the panels and the calendar; the Two Telescopes About surface and the Guide can be slotted alongside since they are mostly content. Compatibility and the daily reading are at or near floor already.

Cadence: one surface built to its floor and validated per session, not several stubbed at once. The regression that cost two months came from building surface by surface with nothing forcing agreement. The discipline now is the opposite: one surface, built to the named basement, validated against the Definition of Done and the core, before the next is opened. A surface is not presented until it passes the gate.
